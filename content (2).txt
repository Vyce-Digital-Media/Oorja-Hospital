PAGE: SERVICES — GASTROENTEROLOGY
============================================================
Section title: Gastroenterology Services

1. Digestive issues
   Diagnosis and management of acidity, gas, bloating, constipation,
   Irritable Bowel Syndrome and other digestive problems.

2. Liver diseases
   Comprehensive evaluation and treatment of fatty liver, hepatitis,
   jaundice, cirrhosis and other liver disorders.

3. Obesity solutions
   Personalized weight management planning including diet, lifestyle,
   medical and endoscopic support for best long-term results.

4. Pancreatobiliary Disorders
   Expert diagnosis and treatment of bile duct and gallstones, acute and
   chronic pancreatitis and their complications.

5. GI Oncology
   Specialized screening and surveillance for early diagnosis and management
   of gastric and colorectal cancers.

6. Advanced Endoscopy / ERCP / EUS
   Minimally invasive diagnosis and treatment of problems in GI tract,
   pancreas, liver and gall bladder with precision and care.

PAGE: SERVICES — FETAL MEDICINE
============================================================
Section title: Fetal Medicine Services

1. 3D/4D Ultrasonography
   Advanced imaging that shows real-life movements and expressions of your
   baby in the womb.

2. Multiple Pregnancy Management
   Detailed monitoring of twin and triplet pregnancies as to ensure a safer
   journey for the mother as well the babies.

3. High Risk Pregnancy Counselling
   Counselling for women requiring special care due to maternal health
   conditions, previous pregnancy complications or fetal concerns.

4. Invasive Fetal Testing Procedures
   Specialized procedure for early diagnosis of genetic and chromosomal
   conditions.

5. Fetal Echocardiography
   Detailed structural and functional evaluation of the baby's heart.

6. Fetal Neurosonography
   Specialized scan of the baby's brain for monitoring neurological
   development.

Eyebrow: About Us
Heading: A dedicated super-speciality centre for digestive health and fetal

Body copy:
Led by Dr Harsh Jain and Dr Ishita Agarwal, the centre focuses on creating a
trusted healthcare environment where patients can receive personalised
attention, evidence based treatment and ethical medical care. From managing
digestive and liver disorders to specialized fetal imaging and high risk
pregnancy evaluation, our goal is to provide modern, reliable and accessible
healthcare under one roof.

Facility highlights (list):
- AIIMS-trained doctors with experience from premiere institutes like Medanta
  and Apollo.
- State-of-the-art Ultrasound Machine for high definition pregnancy
  sonography.
- Endoscopy suite equipped with image enhancement for accurate diagnosis &
  management.
- Daycare facility for endoscopic procedures and treatments requiring minimal
  hospital stay.
- Admission facility for patients requiring hospitalization and advanced
  medical support.
- 24x7 on-call emergency services for patients in need.
